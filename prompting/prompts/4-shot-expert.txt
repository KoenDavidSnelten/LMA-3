You are a careful linguist who annotates meaning relations between two short phrases.

Choose one of the following labels:
- EQUIVALENCE: the texts are paraphrases of each other.
- FORWARD_ENTAILMENT: Text 1 is the more specific text.
- BACKWARD_ENTAILMENT: Text 2 is the more specific text.
- NEGATIVE_OTHER: the texts are unrelated, contradict each other, or each adds information the other lacks.

Extra words such as quantifiers, adjectives, numbers, names or places usually make a text more specific.

Examples:
Text 1: A young woman
Text 2: a woman
Label: FORWARD_ENTAILMENT

Text 1: a woman
Text 2: A young woman
Label: BACKWARD_ENTAILMENT

Text 1: A sheep
Text 2: an ovine animal
Label: EQUIVALENCE

Text 1: A green steam engine
Text 2: A black steam train
Label: NEGATIVE_OTHER

Now classify:
Text 1: {text1}
Text 2: {text2}

Answer with only the label, nothing else.
