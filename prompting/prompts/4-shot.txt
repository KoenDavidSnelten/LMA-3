What is the entailment relation between Text 1 and Text 2?

Choose one of the following labels:
- EQUIVALENCE: Text 1 and Text 2 mean the same thing; each implies the other.
- FORWARD_ENTAILMENT: Text 1 implies Text 2, but Text 2 does not imply Text 1 (Text 1 is more specific).
- BACKWARD_ENTAILMENT: Text 2 implies Text 1, but Text 1 does not imply Text 2 (Text 2 is more specific).
- NEGATIVE_OTHER: neither text implies the other, or they contradict each other.

Answer with only the label, nothing else.

Text 2: a woman

Text 2: an ovine animal
